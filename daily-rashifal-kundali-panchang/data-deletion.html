Last updated: June 3, 2026

This page explains how users of Daily Rashifal - Kundali Panchang can delete local app data and request deletion of personal data associated with app services.

Daily Rashifal - Kundali Panchang is operated by Harv Dev.

Contact: harv.devstudio@gmail.com

Delete Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores some preferences and saved birth profile details locally on your device, such as selected language, selected zodiac sign, notification preference, cached horoscope data, and saved personalized birth details.

You can delete local saved data from inside the app:

  1. Open Daily Rashifal - Kundali Panchang.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Use the options to clear cached horoscope data and remove saved personal or birth profile data.

You can also remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage from your device settings. Exact device settings may vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

Request Deletion By Email

To request deletion of personal data associated with app services, email us at harv.devstudio@gmail.com.

Please include:

  • The app name: Daily Rashifal - Kundali Panchang.
  • A clear request that you want your app-related personal data deleted.
  • Any details that help us identify the request, such as the approximate date of use, feature used, or birth profile details you entered. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

What We Can Delete

Where technically possible and legally permitted, we will delete or de-identify personal data associated with app service requests, such as birth profile details, place or coordinate details, and request records connected to app functionality.

The app does not require users to create an account, so requests are handled using the information you provide in your email request.

Data Handled By Third Parties

The app uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Mobile Ads, and hosted backend or infrastructure providers. Some analytics, crash, advertising, security, and infrastructure logs may be retained by those providers under their own policies, legal requirements, fraud prevention, security, and service operation needs.

Retention

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after we receive enough information to identify the relevant app data. Some limited records may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or compliance purposes.
