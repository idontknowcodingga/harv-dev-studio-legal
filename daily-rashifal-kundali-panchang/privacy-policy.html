Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Rashifal - Kundali Panchang handles information when you use the app.

App Operator

Daily Rashifal - Kundali Panchang is operated by Harv Dev.

Contact: harv.devstudio@gmail.com

Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information depending on which features you use:

  • Language preference and selected zodiac sign.
  • Notification preference for daily reminders.
  • Birth profile details entered by you, such as name, birth date, birth time, birth place, coordinates, and timezone.
  • Current location only when you choose the current-location option for Panchang calculations.
  • App usage and device information through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data through Google Mobile Ads when ads are enabled.
  • Network request metadata such as IP address, timestamps, and technical logs handled by hosted backend or infrastructure providers.

The app does not require an account.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Show localized app screens and astrology content.
  • Calculate or request Rashifal, Kundali, Panchang, and personalized astrology results.
  • Save your local preferences and birth profile so you do not need to re-enter them.
  • Send local daily notification reminders if you enable them.
  • Improve reliability, diagnose crashes, and understand basic app usage.
  • Serve, measure, and protect ads when ads are enabled.
  • Maintain backend security, debugging, rate limiting, and service operation.

Location

The app requests location permission only for features that need location, such as calculating Panchang for your current place. You can also enter or select a place manually instead of using device location.

Local Storage

Some preferences and birth profile details are stored on your device using local app storage. You can clear saved personal data from the app settings. Removing or uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored app data, depending on your device settings.

Sharing And Third Parties

We may process or share information with service providers needed to operate the app:

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for analytics and crash diagnostics.
  • Google Mobile Ads for advertising when ads are enabled.
  • Hosted backend and infrastructure providers for astrology calculations, location search, Panchang, Kundali, Rashifal, request handling, security, and diagnostics.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retention And Deletion

Locally saved profile and preference data remains on your device until you clear it from app settings or remove the app. Backend and third-party service data may be retained according to operational, security, analytics, crash diagnosis, advertising, and legal requirements.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact harv.devstudio@gmail.com.

Children

Security
